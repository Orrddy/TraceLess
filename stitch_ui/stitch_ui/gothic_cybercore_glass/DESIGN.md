## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes high-contrast ecclesiastical geometry with razor-sharp cybernetic precision. Built strictly around a light aesthetic, it merges the sacred architectural verticality of Gothic cathedrals—lancet arches, crisp tracery linework, structural buttresses—with a futuristic cybercore framework defined by frosted optical glass, phosphor accent diodes, and cryptographic metadata displays.

The target audience comprises security researchers, digital archivists, protocol architects, and high-assurance platform engineers who demand extreme utilitarian legibility alongside an uncompromising, avant-garde visual signature. The interface evokes cold digital sanctuary: sacred precision, zero-leak telemetry, mathematical order, and crystalline transparency. 

Key stylistic tensions:
- **Cathedral Structuralism:** Fine-line skeletal borders (1px hairline ink), vertical tension, clipped multi-axis facets, and arched lancet masks alongside modern layout architecture.
- **Luminous Glassmorphism:** Translucent ivory and cool-white substrates suspended over subtle sub-surface circuit traces and microscopic horizontal scanlines.
- **Cybernetic Contrast:** Obsidian-grade black ink text accented by piercing electric blues, high-luminance cyans, and cryptographic violet pulses.

## Colors

The palette operates under an intense high-key light mode discipline. Pure luminous whites and pale atmospheric undertones provide the substrate, deep structural carbon ink grounds the typography, and neon spectrum accents deliver dynamic cybercore telemetry.

### Substrates & Background Surfaces
- **Canvas Root (`#FFFFFF`):** Absolute luminous base canvas.
- **Ivory Veil (`#FAFAF8`):** Warmer micro-surface for deep structural containers and background cards.
- **Cool Mist (`#F4F7FB`):** Elevated frosted panes, panels, and sidebars.
- **Glacial Slate (`#EAF0F7`):** Recessed telemetry channels, troughs, and inactive track fields.

### Ink & Structural Black
- **Deep Obsidian Ink (`#101217`):** Primary display text, high-order headers, and primary solid callouts.
- **Cathedral Charcoal (`#1C1D24`):** Secondary body copy, persistent structural wirelines, and container framing.
- **Muted Carbon Slate (`#4B5563`):** Metadata annotations, timestamp ribbons, and secondary labels.
- **Hairline Tracer (`rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.08)`): The standard architectural perimeter rule.

### Cybercore Signal Accents
- **Electric Blue (`#246BFE`):** Primary interaction trigger, focused input outlines, critical action nodes.
- **Signal Cyan (`#00CFFF`):** Kinetic micro-indicators, real-time sync pulses, and diagnostic telemetry.
- **Digital Violet (`#805CFF`):** Cryptographic verification tags, permission hashes, and tertiary interactive states.

### Cryptographic Security Status
- **Verified Emerald (`#16B981`):** Active ciphering, intact integrity nodes, nominal operation.
- **Entropy Amber (`#D99000`):** Handshake degradation, perimeter warning, key rotation prompt.
- **Breach Coral (`#E44B5F`):** Cryptographic signature fault, invalid access token, critical breach alert.

Transparent overlays leverage strictly balanced RGBA tokens to maintain contrast: Frosted glass panes utilize `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` to `rgba(244, 247, 251, 0.85)` with backdrop filters, avoiding low-contrast illegibility.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is defined by three intentional contrasts:
1. **Dramatic Editorial Display (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers gothic theatricality and architectural verticality. High stroke-contrast, razor serifs, and sharp terminals emulate stone-carved cathedral inscriptions. Reserved exclusively for hero statements, primary section markers, and dramatic modal titles.
2. **Precision Structural Body (`Geist`):** Delivers clean readability. Neutral, mathematically engineered sans-serif forms balance out the dramatic serif titles, ensuring functional data ingestion without cognitive fatigue.
3. **Cryptographic Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`):** Acts as the cybernetic metadata layer. Applied to all technical values, state indicators, coordinates, status markers, system telemetry, and micro badges. Monospace labels should almost always be styled uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`).

Typographic treatments:
- Pair all `headline-lg` and `headline-xl` titles with an uppercase `label-sm` technical prefix (e.g., `// NODE_ID: 0x9482 // CATENARY_ROOT`).
- Numerical counters, memory addresses, and telemetry points must be rendered in `JetBrains Mono` with tabular lining figures enabled.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the structural grid of Gothic architectural drawings crossed with strict integrated circuit schematics.

### Spatial Grid System
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column structural grid with 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters and 3rem (`48px`) margins. Section containers feature visible baseline hairline borders, mimicking draftsmanship construction lines.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 1rem (`16px`) gutters and 2rem (`32px`) margins.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with 0.75rem (`12px`) gutters and 1rem (`16px`) margins. Deep multi-tier columns collapse into vertically rhythmic stacks.

### Structural Scaffolding & Tracery Dividers
Interface panels are aligned to absolute vertical tracks. Instead of arbitrary empty whitespace, structural margins are occasionally defined by 1px subtle guide lines (`rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.06)`) adorned with micro crosshairs (`+`) at terminal intersections. 

Use component padding scale:
- `space-xs` (4px) for compact icon-to-label inline offsets and status pills.
- `space-sm` (8px) for internal pill padding, button edge offsets, and tag bounds.
- `space-md` (16px) for interior glass container cells and card gutters.
- `space-lg` (24px) for major card interiors, dialog shells, and structural section splits.
- `space-xl` (40px) for macro separation between hero banners, telemetry monitors, and card decks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through high-spec frosted glass physical simulation layered over razor-thin structural ink outlines. The system avoids muddy multi-layer shadows, relying instead on ambient white transmission and sharp, directional atmospheric luminescence.

### Glass Stack Recipe (Multi-Layer Translucency)
1. **Base Plate (Elevation 0 - Substrate):** `#FFFFFF` or `#FAFAF8` base canvas, optionally underlaid with a subtle procedural SVG pattern: 0.5px circuit paths (`rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.03)`) and 1px scanline stripes spaced 4px apart (`rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.015)`).
2. **Tier 1 Glass Card (Elevation 1 - Content Shell):**
   - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px) saturate(180%)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.08)`
   - Box Shadow: `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.04), inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`
3. **Tier 2 Floating Pane (Elevation 2 - Menus, Drawers, Flyouts):**
   - Background: `rgba(244, 247, 251, 0.88)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(24px) saturate(200%)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(36, 107, 254, 0.2)`
   - Box Shadow: `0 16px 48px -4px rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.08), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6) inset`
4. **Tier 3 Cryptographic Modal / Overlay (Elevation 3):**
   - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(32px) contrast(105%)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.15)`
   - Box Shadow: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.14), 0 0 0 1px rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.05)`

### Gothic Arch Masking & Dynamic Highlights
- Active and focused glass panes can incorporate a subtle top-edge gradient glow: `linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(36, 107, 254, 0.4), transparent)`.
- Cards designated as "Sanctuary Nodes" feature an inner upper arched lancet mask or an etched 1px dual gothic corner flourish.

## Shapes

The design system uses a restrained structural roundedness rating of `1` (Soft: 0.25rem / 4px base radius) across interactive elements, paired with specialized architectural lancet geometry for macro features.

### Corner Rules
- **Interactive Units (Buttons, Chips, Inputs):** 4px (`0.25rem`) fixed corner radius. This prevents the interface from becoming bubbly, retaining a disciplined, mechanical feel.
- **Surface Cards & Trays:** 8px (`0.5rem`) outer boundary.
- **Modals & Master Frames:** 12px (`0.75rem`) outer boundary.
- **Architectural Variant ("Cathedral Chamfer"):** Selected hero containers, diagnostic dialogs, and primary panels substitute radial corners with a `6px` 45-degree corner cut (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) to mimic carved stone capitals and technical aerospace bezels.

### Tracery & Cathedral Lancet Geometries
- Feature hero cards and visual focal points may employ a Gothic Lancet Arch profile (`border-radius: 9999px 9999px 4px 4px` on top-level frame caps) or fine 1px black circular inner arches within SVG decorative headers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber Arch Button:** High-contrast solid deep obsidian `#101217` fill, white `#FFFFFF` text in `JetBrains Mono` (12px, tracking `0.08em`, uppercase). Border: 1px solid `#101217`. On hover: subtle background shift to `#1C1D24` accompanied by a sharp `0 0 16px rgba(36, 107, 254, 0.35)` outer neon corona.
- **Secondary Frosted Glass Button:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` background with backdrop blur `12px`. Text in charcoal `#1C1D24`. Border: 1px hairline `rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.12)`. On hover: border switches to Electric Blue `#246BFE`, background becomes `#F4F7FB`.
- **Ghost Cryptographic Link:** Transparent surface, `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase text in `#4B5563`, bordered with empty bracket glyphs: `[ EXECUTE_QUERY ]`. On hover: text turns to `#246BFE`.

### Input Fields
- Container: White glass pane `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `4px` radius. Height `40px`.
- Typography: Input value in `Geist` 14px; prefix labels or parameter markers in `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase slate `#4B5563`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.1)`.
- State: On focus, border snaps to Electric Blue `#246BFE` with `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px rgba(36, 107, 254, 0.15)`. Active inputs display a pulsing Cyan `#00CFFF` vertical insertion carat.

### Cards & Sanctuary Panels
- Body: Built on Tier 1 or Tier 2 glass recipes. 
- Headers: Divided from card content by an ultra-thin 1px horizontal rule (`rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.06)`). Headers combine an editorial title (`Bodoni Moda`, 20px) on the left with a mono tracking code (`JetBrains Mono`, 10px, e.g., `HEX // 0048F`) on the right.
- Embellishments: Optional top-right and bottom-left hairline crosshair markers (`+`) extending 4px outside the border outline.

### Security Status Chips & Tags
- Compact height (`22px`), 2px radius, inline-flex with `0.5rem` horizontal padding.
- Mono typography (`JetBrains Mono`, 10px, bold, uppercase).
- Variants:
  - *Intact (Verified):* Pale emerald tint `rgba(22, 185, 129, 0.08)`, border `rgba(22, 185, 129, 0.25)`, text `#0D6848`, with a solid emerald `#16B981` 5px circular diode on the left.
  - *Degraded (Amber):* Pale amber tint `rgba(217, 144, 0, 0.08)`, border `rgba(217, 144, 0, 0.25)`, text `#8A5C00`, with a pulsing 5px amber diode.
  - *Compromised (Coral):* Pale coral tint `rgba(228, 75, 95, 0.08)`, border `rgba(228, 75, 95, 0.3)`, text `#B32638`, with a blinking 5px coral diode.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box: 16px by 16px square with 2px radius. Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`. Border: 1px hairline `#1C1D24`.
- Checked State: Deep Obsidian `#101217` fill with a crisp vector checkmark rendered in bright Signal Cyan `#00CFFF`.
- Radios: 16px outer circle, selecting initiates a sharp diamond-cut center dot in Electric Blue `#246BFE`.

### Telemetry Lists & Data Grids
- Rows: Transparent backgrounds with alternating subtle zebra striping (`rgba(244, 247, 251, 0.4)`).
- Separators: 1px continuous dotted line (`rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.08)`).
- Hover Row: Highlighted in `rgba(36, 107, 254, 0.04)` with an electric-blue 2px vertical indicator bar pinned to the extreme left border.

### Cryptographic Watermark & Terminal Ribbon (Domain Component)
- Bottom-pinned or sidebar metadata ribbon displaying persistent verification hashes: `SHA-256: e3b0c442...` in `label-sm` font, slate `#4B5563`, framed with subtle horizontal SVG scanlines to ground the cybercore theme.
