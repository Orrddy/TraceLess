# Surface brief — TraceLess marketing home + authenticated shell

## Scope and mode

- Primary target: `/` (Persuade).
- Related target: `/dashboard` and the shared app shell (Operate).
- This pass: establish the replacement visual world from the owner's pinned brief, rebuild the homepage intro/hero and the authenticated shell + overview dashboard, and make every shared token/component render coherently in that world. Inner Livewire screens inherit the shell treatment.

## Audience, job, action

- Visitor: privacy-conscious individual arriving with a specific exposure worry.
- Job: decide whether TraceLess can find their public footprint and give them a real path to reduce it.
- Action: start a scan (register), or explore how verification and removal work.
- Proof available: real verification evidence model, public-data-only policy, consent gate, WhatsMyName attribution, demo/live labelling. No testimonials, customer logos, or removal-success claims.
- Constraints: never imply a breach provider is active; never imply removal is guaranteed; demo data must be visibly labelled.

## Chosen direction

Brief-pinned: a private intelligence operations surface. Obsidian and graphite fields, electric cyan reserved for active state and measurement, editorial sans display, precise monospace telemetry. Cinematic real video opens the experience; the product's own verification evidence is the proof, not decorative terminal theatre.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make public exposure feel measurable and answerable. The page opens as a classified briefing played over the owner's real footage, then hands the visitor a working verification record — the category's vague "privacy score" hero and stock dashboard-card grid are refused.

OWN-WORLD: Obsidian `#080A0D`, graphite `#101419`, elevated `#171D24`, silver `#A6B0BC`, ink `#F1F4F7`; cyan `#55D6E8` only on active state, focus and measurement; emerald/amber/red strictly semantic. Hairline rules instead of card borders; sharp 12px corners; fine 48px grid with a single scan sweep; Schibsted Grotesk display, Inter body, JetBrains Mono for identifiers and telemetry.

STORY: The visitor understands within the first viewport that TraceLess looks only at public data, verifies what it finds, and gives them a removal path; they register because the evidence model is more credible than another alert feed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed video with a bottom-weighted obsidian gradient. A thin telemetry strip sits under the fixed navigation (classification, protocol, node). Left, at 55% width: a small boxed confidentiality stamp, the headline "Your Digital Footprint. Under Your Control." at 4.5–6rem, one sentence of plain copy, primary "Scan my digital footprint" and secondary "See how verification works". Right: the video subject remains visible. Bottom edge: three quiet guarantees in one row. Sound is opt-in, skip is always present, and the intro never blocks the page.

FORM: brief-pinned operating-terminal world, not rolled; `seed: brief-pinned`. Composition favours left-aligned briefing copy over a cinematic field, with the evidence record as the recurring object across marketing and product.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
